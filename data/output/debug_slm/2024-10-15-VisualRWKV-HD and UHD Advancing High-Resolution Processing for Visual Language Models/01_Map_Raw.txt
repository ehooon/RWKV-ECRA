- VisualRWKV-HD and UHD are introduced as advancements in the VisualRWKV model family for high-resolution visual processing.
- Accurate visual information understanding is crucial for VLMs to reduce hallucinations and improve performance in tasks like text-rich or document analysis.
- Enhancing image resolution improves visual perception but can increase computational demands and input sequence length.
- VisualRWKV-HD uses a lossless downsampling method to integrate a high-resolution vision encoder with low-resolution encoders without extending the input sequence length.
- The model supports resolutions up to 1024 x 1024, achieving strong results on VLM benchmarks, especially in text-rich tasks.
- VisualRWKV-UHD divides the image into four segments, recombines them with the original image, and supports resolutions up to 4096 x 4096 while keeping image tokens under 1024.
- Both models use an MLP with Context Gating to replace linear projection layers, stabilizing training and improving performance.
- High-resolution vision encoders are aligned with low-resolution modules by combining every 2x2 block into a new channel dimension without additional training.
- Comprehensive experiments on eight benchmarks demonstrate effectiveness, particularly in text-rich tasks.

- 原文核心事实提取：
- VisualRWKV-HD 使用预训练的 SAM 视觉编码器，支持 1024x1024 高分辨率图像，提升模型性能。
- 采用损失less下采样方法：将 2x2 块图像（每个包含四个相邻向量）通过公式 C_new = Concat(C1, C2, C3, C4) 合并到新通道维度，有效保留图像细节。
- VisualRWKV-UHD 将图像分割为四部分，分别用 SigLip、DINOv2、SAM 编码器处理，通过 avgpool2d 汇总后与低分辨率特征融合，支持 4096x4096 高分辨率输入。
- VisualRWKV-UHD 引入 MLP With Context Gating：公式 y = σ(W_g x + b_g) ⊗ x，其中 y 为输出，W_g、b_g 为门控权重和偏置，σ 为 sigmoid 激活函数，⊗ 表示逐元素乘法。该机制动态调整特征信息，稳定训练并提升性能。
- VisualRWKV-HD/UHD 相比 LLaVA-UHD、VisualRWKV 在高分辨率图像处理中更高效，细节保留更好。

## 局限性
- VisualRWKV-HD 和 VisualRWKV-UHD 模型存在高计算成本和内存需求，可能限制对硬件较弱用户的可访问性，特别是影响实时应用。
- 模型依赖训练数据的质量和数量；高质量标注数据的有限可用性可能损害其有效性。
- 决策过程缺乏可解释性，在医疗保健和金融等关键领域构成挑战。
- 尽管在基准测试中表现出色，但模型在多样化和未见过场景中的泛化能力需要进一步验证。
- 将模型与其他模态（如音频或触觉）集成仍是一个待探索的领域，这可能限制其在多模态学习背景下的应用范围。

- **Model Architecture and Computing**
- **Model Architecture**: VisualRWKV models are visual extensions of the Recurrent Weighted Key-Value (RWKV) architecture, designed for visual and textual tasks.
- VisualRWKV 1.6B: Baseline model with 1.6 billion parameters.
- VisualRWKV 1.6B + MLP: Enhanced with a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) for improved feature extraction.
- VisualRWKV 1.6B + MLP (HD/UHD): Models using High Definition (HD) and Ultra High Definition (UHD) strategies for fine-grained feature extraction.
- **Computing Infrastructure**:
- Standard training and benchmark evaluation used 8 NVIDIA A100-80GB GPUs.
- VisualRWKV 7B model trained with 6 A100 GPUs due to memory constraints with 8 GPUs.
- Efficiency analysis used an NVIDIA RTX 3090 GPU.
- **Computing Budget**:
- Training an epoch of VisualRWKV 1.6B with 8 A100 GPUs takes 6.7 hours, equivalent to 53.6 GPU hours.
- Training an epoch of VisualRWKV 3B with 8 A100 GPUs takes 11.3 hours, equivalent to 90.4 GPU hours.
- Training an epoch of VisualRWKV 7B with 6 A100 GPUs takes 26.5 hours, equivalent to 159 GPU hours.
- **Training Details**: RWKV backbone adapted for visual tasks by incorporating Vision Encoders and using Context Gating; fine-tuned for visual question-answering on datasets like DocVQA, InfographicVQA, and ChartQA.
- **Datasets**
- **mix665k**: Dataset used by LLaVA for instruction tuning, comprising 665,000 diverse images to enhance model adaptability and performance in various visual tasks and instructions.
- **HD559k**: Custom high-resolution dataset with 559,000 high-quality images, focusing on testing model performance in detail, color, and clarity; data proportions shown in Figure 3 (total 559,494 images).
- **HD667k**: Another high-resolution dataset with 667,000 images; enriches training data for diverse visual scenarios, improving generalization ability and robustness; data proportions shown in Figure 4 (total 667,000 images).
- **Experimental Setup**
- **Preprocessing**: Input images divided into four sections; each encoded by three vision encoders (SigLIP, DINOv2, SAM); features merged and passed through an MLP with Context Gating.
- **Training**: Models trained using AdamW optimizer with a learning rate of X on NVIDIA GPUs with mixed precision; 100 epochs with early stopping after 10 epochs of no improvement.
- **Evaluation**: Models evaluated on DocVQA, InfographicVQA, and ChartQA datasets for document understanding, infographics, and chart analysis respectively.

- 训练数据分为两个阶段：
- 特征对齐阶段：使用558K张LAION-CC-SBU图像，连接冻结的视觉编码器和冻结的LLM。
- 视觉指令调优阶段：使用150K个由GPT生成的多模态示例和515K个VQA数据集，增强模型的多模态任务能力。
- 所有数据使用均符合其预期用途。
- 在数据准备中，通过自动化工具和人工审查识别和处理PII和敏感内容，应用数据匿名化技术（如数据掩码）以确保数据完整性和隐私。
- 评估基准包括：
- VQA-v2和GQA：基于test-dev分割评估。
- TextVQA：在验证集上评估。
- ScienceQA和POPE：从各自的测试集评估。
- MMBench：基于开发集评估。
- MME：在特定测试集上评估。
- 训练数据语言分布：
- 大多数VQA数据集为英语。
- ShareGPT数据为多语言，涵盖用户贡献的多种语言。
- 评估基准中，MMBench-cn为中文，其余为英文。
- 模型参数：实验使用1.6B参数进行。
- 详细超参数（针对视觉指令调优和视觉指令调优阶段）列于表10，包括针对不同任务和多样化数据集优化的设置，以确保模型在各种任务中的鲁棒性能。

- MMBench metrics are based on the development set.
- MME is evaluated on a specific test set.
- Training data spans multiple datasets, with most Visual Question Answering (VQA) datasets in English.
- ShareGPT data is multilingual, covering multiple user-contributed languages.
- Evaluation benchmarks include MMBench-cn in Chinese and the rest in English.
